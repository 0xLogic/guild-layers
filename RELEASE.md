Guild Layers 1.8.0

Gold reporting is automatic while the addon is enabled, with no individual sharing toggle. This character's carried gold is visible to guild members using the addon.

The roster now has a reported-gold column and a fresh-report total below the list. The Gold mini-bar button and /guildlayers gold open details with ages and coverage.

Money changes are coalesced at five seconds; periodic refresh is 30 seconds. Reports older than 90 seconds are excluded from totals and expire after five minutes. Offline/missing members are unknown, never zero. Bank, mail, auction proceeds and unreported alts are excluded. Reports are not saved to disk or certified by the server.
